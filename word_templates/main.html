THESIS MASTER SETUP GUIDE

UNIVERSITY OF HARIPUR FYP REPORT

Use these settings in Microsoft Word before assembling the thesis. The chapter and front-matter templates supplied by the portal are designed to be pasted into a document that follows this configuration.

Page Setup

Required document settings
SettingRequired value
Paper sizeA4, portrait, one-sided
MarginsTop 1 inch, bottom 1 inch, left 1.25 inches, right 1 inch
Body fontTimes New Roman, 12 pt
Line spacingDouble
Page-number positionHeader, right aligned, with no visible header rule

Heading Styles

  • Chapter heading: centered and bold, showing “CHAPTER” plus the chapter number on one line and the chapter title below it.
  • Section, subsection, and subsubsection headings: 12 pt Times New Roman, bold, numbered, and left aligned.
  • Front-matter headings: centered, bold, and unnumbered.

Document Order

  1. Title Page
  2. Abstract
  3. Certificate
  4. Declaration
  5. Plagiarism Certificate
  6. Table of Contents
  7. List of Figures
  8. List of Tables
  9. Chapters 1 through 6
  10. Appendix
  11. References

Word Field Setup

  • Use lower-case Roman numerals for front-matter pages, then restart page numbering with Arabic numerals at Chapter 1.
  • Apply Word’s built-in Heading styles to chapter and section headings before inserting the automatic Table of Contents.
  • Use Word captions for figures and tables, then generate the List of Figures and List of Tables from the References tab.
  • Insert page breaks between major front-matter items, chapters, the appendix, and references.
